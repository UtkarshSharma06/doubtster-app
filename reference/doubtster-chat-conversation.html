Physics projectile motion

Kicked at 20 m/s, 30°: height, time, range

Split the speed into sideways and upward parts. Gravity only slows the upward part.

max height5.10m
time in air2.04s
range35.3m
  1. Split the velocity. vₓ = 20 cos30° = 17.32 vᵧ = 20 sin30° = 10
  2. Time to the top (vᵧ = 0): t = 10 / 9.8 = 1.02 s
  3. Height: H = vᵧ² / 2g = 5.10 m   Air time: T = 2 × 1.02 = 2.04 s
  4. Range: R = vₓ × T = 17.32 × 2.04 = 35.3 m

🧭 Reply rules for the Doubtster AI (for the build)

small talk

Greetings, feelings, thanks, jokes. Reply like a friendly person in 1 to 3 sentences. No headings, no answer card, no video, flashcard or quiz buttons.

new question

A new problem or topic. Full structured answer: result first, steps, a graph if it helps. Offer video, flashcards and quiz once, at the end.

clarify a step

"Why…?", "how did you get…?" about part of an earlier answer. Answer only that part, point to the step, reuse its numbers. 3 to 6 sentences.

what if

A changed condition. Keep what stays the same, recalculate only what changes, and show the difference from the first answer.

explain differently

"I don't get it." Never repeat the same wording. Switch method: an analogy, a picture, smaller steps or simpler words. End with one check question.

asked again

The same question as before. Give the result in one line and link back to where it was worked out. Offer a new practice question instead of re-pasting.

  answer. Offer a new practice question.
