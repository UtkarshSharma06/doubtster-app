## 2. How to cancel {#how-to-cancel}

**2.1 Cancel anytime.** You can cancel from your account settings or by emailing contact@doubtster.com. We will confirm your cancellation by email. If you do not receive a confirmation, contact us before your next charge date.

**2.2 What cancelling does.** Cancelling stops future charges. It does not refund a charge that has already been made.

**2.3 If you cancel during the trial.** You will not be charged the plan price. You keep your trial credits until the trial ends. The trial fee is not refundable.

**2.4 If you cancel after your plan has started.** Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again. Plan credits can be used until they expire.

**2.5 Know your charge date.** Your account shows your next charge date. Cancel before that date. Because payments can take time to process, we recommend cancelling at least 24 hours before it.

**2.6 Deleting is not cancelling.** Deleting your account, uninstalling an app, or simply no longer using the Service does not cancel your plan. You must cancel it.

## 3. When you are charged {#when-charged}

**3.1 Trial fee.** The trial fee is charged when you start the trial.

**3.2 Your plan.** Your chosen plan starts automatically when the 7-day trial ends, and we charge the plan price at that time. After that, we charge you each month (Monthly plan) or each year (Annual plan) on the anniversary of the date your plan started, until you cancel.

**3.3 Top-ups.** Top-ups are charged when you buy them.

**3.4 Receipts and reminders.** We email a receipt for each payment. Where the law requires it, we also send a reminder before your trial ends or before an annual renewal. Not receiving or not reading an email does not by itself entitle you to a refund, except where the law says otherwise.

**3.5 Where to see prices and dates.** Prices are shown at checkout, and your next charge date is shown in your account.

## 4. No refunds for forgotten cancellations {#forgotten-cancellations}

> **Important:** If you were charged because you did not cancel in time, the charge is not refundable.

**4.1 What this covers.** We do not refund a charge that happens because you did not cancel before your trial ended or before a renewal date. This includes:

- forgetting to turn off auto-renew or autopay;
- forgetting that your trial would turn into a paid plan;
- not noticing, or not reading, our emails or reminders;
- not realising you would be charged, after you accepted the terms shown at checkout; and
- not using your credits, or not using the Service at all.

**4.2 How to avoid it.** Set your own reminder, and cancel before your trial ends or before your renewal date.

**4.3 Limits.** This section does not apply where the law gives you a right to a refund (see section 8), or in the situations listed in section 6, such as when you cancelled correctly but were still charged.

## 5. Payments are not refundable {#no-refunds}

**5.1 General rule.** Except as set out in sections 6 and 8, all payments are final and not refundable. This includes:

- the trial fee;
- plan payments, including a Monthly or Annual plan that has started;
- top-ups;
- partly used billing periods, unused credits and expired credits;
- a change of mind, or finding that the Service is not what you expected;
- dissatisfaction with an answer or video, because AI can make mistakes (please use the "Report a mistake" button); and
- an account that we suspend or end because you broke our Terms.

**5.2 Credits.** Credits have no cash value, and we do not convert credits into money.

## 6. When we will refund {#when-we-refund}

**6.1 Refund cases.** We will refund the affected amount, or fix the problem if that is better, in these cases:

- **(a) Payment verification failure.** You were charged, but your payment failed verification (for example, a failed bank or card authentication check), or your plan or credits were never activated.
- **(b) Duplicate or mistaken charge.** You were charged twice for the same purchase, or charged an amount that does not match the price shown at checkout.
- **(c) Charge after cancellation.** You cancelled correctly before the charge date, but you were still charged.
- **(d) Account or technical bug on our side.** A bug in our systems meant you could not use a plan or credits you paid for, for example your account or credits were not set up after payment, or a video could not be generated because of a fault on our side, and we could not fix it within a reasonable time.
- **(e) Unauthorised payment.** Someone used your payment method without your permission and you tell us promptly.
- **(f) Service ended by us.** We end your account for a reason other than your breaking our Terms, or we stop providing the Service. We refund the unused part of any plan you have prepaid.
- **(g) Where the law requires.** See section 8.

**6.2 Fix first.** For cases (a) and (d), we will usually try to fix the problem first, for example by activating your plan or restoring your credits. If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund the affected amount.

**6.3 How much.** We refund the amount that was charged in error or that relates to service you could not use. We may reduce a refund by the value of any credits you have already used.

**6.4 Evidence and abuse.** We may ask for your account email, a receipt and screenshots. We may decline a request if we find evidence of fraud or abuse of this policy, such as repeated or false claims.

## 7. How to request a refund {#how-to-request}

**7.1 Send us the details.** Email contact@doubtster.com within 14 days of the charge. Please include your account email, the date and amount of the charge, your receipt or transaction number, what went wrong, and any screenshots.

**7.2 Our reply.** We aim to reply within 24 to 48 hours. We will tell you our decision, and our reasons, by email.

**7.3 Payment of refunds.** An approved refund goes back to the original payment method, usually through our payment provider. It can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

**7.4 Late requests.** We may decline requests made more than 14 days after the charge, except where the law requires otherwise or where we agree the problem could not reasonably have been found earlier.

## 9. Disputes and chargebacks {#disputes}

**9.1 Contact us first.** Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can often fix a problem faster than a bank can.

**9.2 If you dispute a valid charge.** If you dispute a charge that is valid under this policy, we may suspend your account while the dispute is reviewed. We may share records with our payment provider and your bank, such as your acceptance of the terms at checkout, our receipts and reminder emails, and your use of the Service.

**9.3 If the dispute is decided in your favour.** We may end access to the plan and credits linked to that payment.
